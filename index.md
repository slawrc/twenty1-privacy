# TWENTY1 — Privacy Policy                            
                                                                                            
  **Effective date:** 2026-05-06                                                            
  **Last updated:** 2026-05-06                                                            
                                                                                            
  ## Plain-language summary                             
                                                                                          
  TWENTY1 is an offline blackjack trainer. **The app does not collect, store, or transmit   
  any personal data off your device.** It contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs,
  no crash reporters, and no tracking of any kind.                                          
                                                        
  The only data that leaves your device is:                                                 
  - **In-app purchase transactions**, handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit. Apple — not the
  developer — receives your payment information.                                            
  - **Apple iCloud Key-Value Storage**, used only to sync your "TWENTY1 Pro" purchase status
   across your own iCloud-signed-in devices. The sync is between you and Apple; the         
  developer never sees it.                              
                                                                                            
  That's it.                                            
                                                                                          
  ## Data stored on your device

  TWENTY1 saves the following to your device's local storage (`UserDefaults`) so the game   
  remembers your progress:
                                                                                            
  - Your in-game balance, level, XP, and rank                                               
  - Your hand history (last 50 hands)                                                     
  - Your settings (sound, haptics, hint preferences)                                        
  - Your username (the one you set in the app — this stays on your device)                  
  - Your equipped cosmetics and which items you own                                         
                                                                                            
  This data **never leaves your device**. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently.      
                                                                                            
  ## Data synced to your iCloud (if signed in)                                              
                                                                                          
  A single boolean flag — whether you have purchased "TWENTY1 Pro" — is stored in Apple's   
  iCloud Key-Value Storage so that your Pro purchase carries over to your other Apple
  devices signed in to the same Apple ID. No other data is synced.                          
                                                        
  This sync is handled by Apple's iCloud service. The developer of TWENTY1 has no access to 
  your iCloud data.
                                                                                            
  ## In-app purchases                                   
                                                                                          
  TWENTY1 offers a single one-time purchase, "TWENTY1 Pro" ($4.99), which unlocks cosmetic  
  items (chip skins, card backs, avatar frames). The purchase is processed by Apple's
  StoreKit. The developer receives only:                                                    
  - A confirmation that the purchase was made (so the cosmetics can be unlocked)
  - Apple's standard aggregated sales reports (which do not identify individual buyers)     
                                                                                            
  The developer never sees your name, email, payment method, or any other personal          
  information related to your purchase.                                                     
                                                                                            
  ## Third parties                                                                        
                                                                                          
  TWENTY1 uses **no third-party services**. No analytics. No ads. No crash reporting. No    
  social-login. No push notifications.
                                                                                            
  The only external services the app interacts with are Apple's StoreKit (for in-app        
  purchases) and Apple's iCloud (for the Pro-flag sync described above).                  
                                                                                            
  ## Children's privacy                                 
                                                                                          
  TWENTY1 simulates blackjack, a casino card game. The App Store age rating is **17+**. The 
  app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect any
   data from children.                                                                      
                                                        
  If a parent or guardian believes their child has used the app, no action is required:     
  nothing is collected, so there is nothing to delete.
                                                                                            
  ## Your rights                                        
                                                                                          
  Because no personal data is collected, there is no data to access, modify, export, or     
  delete on the developer's side. To remove all app data from your device, simply delete the
   app — iOS will purge all `UserDefaults` and local storage automatically.                 
                                                        
  To remove the Pro-flag from your iCloud after deleting the app, you can sign out of iCloud
   or use **Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage** to clear app data.

  If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the new revision. 
  Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
