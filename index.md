# TWENTY1 — Privacy Policy
  
  **Effective date:** 2026-05-17
  **Last updated:** 2026-05-17
  
  ## Plain-language summary
  
  TWENTY1 is an offline blackjack trainer. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data
  off your device. It has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporters, and no tracking.
  
  The only data leaving the device is your in-app purchase activity through Apple StoreKit and, if you opt
  in, your Game Center leaderboard score.
  
  ## Data stored on your device
  
  Local storage (`UserDefaults`) preserves:
  
  - Your in-game balance, level, XP, and rank
  - Hand history and session summaries
  - Settings, username, and equipped cosmetics
  - Drill Mode and Heat-Map training stats
  - Which Learn pages you have read
  
  This data never leaves your device.
  
  ## Game Center
  
  TWENTY1 uses Apple's Game Center to display an optional leaderboard for lifetime chip balance. If you are
  signed into Game Center on your device, the app submits your lifetime balance as a score whenever a
  session ends. This score is handled entirely by Apple's Game Center service and is governed by Apple's
  privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in iOS Settings to opt out. The developer of
  TWENTY1 has no direct access to your Game Center account data — only the aggregated leaderboard rankings
  that Apple makes publicly visible.
  
  ## In-app purchases

  TWENTY1 Pro ($2.99 one-time, no subscription) adds Drill Mode and the Deviation Heat-Map training tools
  and unlocks cosmetic items (chip skins, card backs, avatar frames). Purchases are processed by Apple
  StoreKit. The developer receives purchase confirmations and aggregated sales reports only — never personal
   buyer information.
  
  ## iCloud
  
  TWENTY1 does not use iCloud for any data sync in v1. (Cross-device Pro restoration is planned for a future
   version.)
  
  ## Third parties
  
  TWENTY1 uses no third-party services. Only Apple's StoreKit and Game Center interact with the app.
  
  ## Children's privacy

  App rating: 17+. The app is not directed at children under 13.

  ## Your rights

  Because no personal data is collected by the developer, there is no data to access, modify, export, or
  delete. Deleting the app removes all local data. Game Center scores can be managed through iOS Settings →
  Game Center.
  
  ## Changes to this policy
  
  Policy updates will appear in the "Last updated" date and the app's release notes.
